# Donate

## Mode

Persuade

## Job

Help a Montlake family understand what a gift supports, trust the organization,
and choose a convenient giving method without pressure or ambiguity.

## Direction

**Gift Journey.** Begin with the student experience, show how support travels
through staffing, programs, and access, then compare giving methods and close
with equity and nonprofit trust.

## Proof Sequence

1. Real Montlake student-program photography.
2. Staffing is 75–80% of the PTA budget.
3. Student programs and family access benefit throughout the year.
4. One-time, recurring, employer match, volunteer-hour match, and check options.
5. 501(c)(3) status and Federal Tax ID.

## First Viewport

The page states the purpose in plain language, shows real student work, and
offers one primary online donation action plus a secondary employer-match path.

## Constraints

- Donation amounts do not determine community belonging.
- Do not invent donor counts, outcomes, urgency, or matching rates.
- Preserve PayPal, Benevity, check, and fundraising email destinations.
- Use the Montlake Field Guide visual system.
