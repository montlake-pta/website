# Montlake PTA Website

## Platform

Web

## Primary Users

Montlake Elementary parents and caregivers, including:

- returning families who need a date, contact, or school-day detail quickly;
- new and prospective families learning how Montlake works;
- volunteers and community members looking for ways to participate;
- PTA editors who continue to author source content in Wix.

Most visits are brief, practical, and likely to happen on a phone between other
family responsibilities.

## Purpose

The website is the public field guide for the Montlake Elementary community. It
helps families find reliable school information, understand PTA programs, stay
connected, and choose how to participate.

## Positioning

Montlake is a small neighborhood school with unusually strong family, staff,
arts, garden, inclusion, and volunteer traditions. The site should feel like a
capable neighbor who knows where to find the answer—not a corporate nonprofit,
an athletics site, or a generic school template.

## Operating Context

- Wix remains the authoring and data source for Blog, Events, Stores, and CMS
  collections.
- GitHub Actions synchronizes Wix data and generates a static GitHub Pages site.
- Content must remain useful when products, events, or optional CMS records are
  absent.
- Families may arrive through old URLs, search results, newsletters, or direct
  links to individual pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve working external services for membership, donations, newsletters,
  enrichment registration, calendar subscriptions, and seasonal fundraising.
- Do not expose Wix or GitHub credentials in browser code.
- Prefer static, resilient, accessible output over unnecessary client-side
  complexity.
- Keep important information readable at mobile sizes and browser zoom.
- Treat dates, contacts, school hours, and program instructions as operational
  information, not decorative copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official Montlake wolf mark and school photography.
- Current school and PTA content maintained in Wix.
- Live Google Calendar and existing service links.
- Public 2026–27 board, program, event, and family information.

## Brand Commitments

- Welcoming without being sentimental.
- Specific to Montlake rather than interchangeable with another school.
- Practical before promotional.
- Inclusive of families with different schedules, resources, abilities, and
  levels of familiarity with the school.
- Transparent about funding, participation, and who to contact.

## Design Principles

1. Put the family’s likely task before organizational storytelling.
2. Use community evidence—people, programs, dates, and contacts—rather than
   invented claims.
3. Make long pages navigable and short visits successful.
4. Let the wolf identity inform color and geometry without turning the site into
   athletics branding.
5. Prefer clear hierarchy, generous reading space, and strong focus states over
   decorative containers.

## Accessibility Needs

- WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Visible keyboard focus and logical document order.
- Touch targets appropriate for mobile use.
- Readable type at browser zoom and with long or translated content.
- Reduced-motion behavior that removes nonessential movement without hiding
  state.
