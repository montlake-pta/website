Why we fundraise

Your donation helps provide programs, staffing, and experiences that enrich the school day for every Montlake student. Public funding does not cover everything our students need. Together, Montlake families help fill that gap.

Every family’s circumstances are different. Please give at the level that works for your family: gifts of all sizes add up to more support and opportunities for our students. We are aiming for 100% participation.

How to give

  1. Online, one-time or recurring. Give through the Fall Fundraiser giving page, and share it with family, friends, and neighbors.
  2. Employer matching. Many companies, including Google, Microsoft, Apple, Symetra, the Gates Foundation, T-Mobile, and Alaska Airlines, match donations through Benevity or their own portals. Some also match volunteer hours. Check with your HR department.
  3. Payroll deduction. Several employers let you give directly from your paycheck and apply matching funds automatically.
  4. Check. Make checks payable to “Montlake PTA Fall Fundraiser.” Send them through backpack mail, or mail them to Montlake PTA, 2025 E Calhoun Street, Seattle, WA 98112.

For year-round options, see all ways to give.

Coffee at drop-off

The PTA is serving coffee, bagels, and treats for parents at drop-off on Monday, October 12; Friday, October 23; and Friday, November 6.

Questions

Email PTA fundraising volunteers Kelsey Miller and Ewa Sack at fundraising@montlakepta.org. Read how the PTA budget works, or see the Fall 2025 campaign archive.

Supporting equity

Montlake PTA also leads advocacy for equitable funding across schools. This year you may also choose to give to Montlake PTA’s Equity Fund, which supports schools such as Lowell Elementary and the SE Seattle Schools Fundraising Alliance that lack PTA fundraising resources.
